Creator Toolkit

Everything you need to plan, script and post game guides without staring at a blank page.

1. Guide video script template

Use for any tutorial or collectible guide. Aim for 3 to 6 minutes.

  1. Hook (0:00 to 0:10). State the result: "Here is how to find every [thing] in [game] without wasting an hour."
  2. Proof (0:10 to 0:25). Show the finished result or the count on screen, so viewers know the guide works.
  3. Route (main body). Walk through the items in the order a player would actually collect them. Put the item count on screen as you go.
  4. Mistakes (last third). Show the one or two things players commonly miss and how to avoid them.
  5. Close (last 15 seconds). Point to the next video and tell viewers where they can get your checklist.

2. Hook formulas for Shorts and TikTok

Fill the brackets, then test two versions of the same clip.

  • "You are missing [number] [items] in [game]. Here is the fastest way to find them."
  • "Do not start [mission or area] until you have done this."
  • "I tracked every [item] in [game] so you do not have to."
  • "[Number] mistakes that cost players [reward] in [game]."
  • "The easiest way to reach 100% in [game], step by step."
  • "I found this by accident in [game] and nobody is talking about it."

3. Thumbnail checklist

  • One clear subject, large enough to read on a phone.
  • Three words of text or fewer, with strong contrast against the background.
  • A number in the image when the video is a count or list.
  • One bright accent color that matches your channel, used on every thumbnail.
  • Only game footage you recorded yourself. Do not copy publisher key art or logos.
  • Check it at the size of a postage stamp. If you cannot read it, simplify.
